Article 19 of the GPSR

Four things every online offer has to indicate.

The regulation lists them as points (a) to (d). Here is what each one asks for, and what the app does about it.

a

The name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the manufacturer, and its postal and electronic address.

You fill this in once. If you make what you sell, the app offers to take it straight from the business details already on your site.

b

Where the manufacturer is not established in the Union, the name, address and contact details of the responsible person.

The app asks for one only when it is needed. A manufacturer established in the Union holds the role itself, and the block says so instead of leaving it blank.

c

Information allowing the product to be identified, including a picture of it, its type and any other product identifier.

The offer's own picture and name already do most of this. Where you have a type, a model or a batch number, you can add it per product.

d

Any warning or safety information to be affixed on the product or accompanying it, in a language easily understood by consumers.

Write a set of warnings once and apply it to a collection or to a handful of products. If your shop sells in more than one language, the app asks for those words in each of them and tells you which are still missing.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what the Wix app GPSR Product Safety Info does with data. It is written for the site owners who install it, and for the people who visit their shops. Throughout, "we" means the developer of the app, whose details are in section 9.

The short version. The app has no server of its own. Everything it saves is written to your own Wix site, into CMS collections that belong to you. We never receive a copy and we never see your data. The app collects nothing at all about the people who visit your shop: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

1

What the app saves, and where

The app creates four CMS collections on your site and writes only to those.

CollectionWhat it holds
GPSR: manufacturers and EU responsible personsThe name, address, e-mail and website of the manufacturers and EU responsible persons you enter
GPSR: product and collection exceptionsWhich of the above applies to which product or collection
GPSR: warning sheetsThe safety warnings, the safety information, their translations, and the hazard pictograms you tick
GPSR: product identificationThe type or model and the batch number you enter for a product

These collections live on your Wix site, under your Wix account. You can open them at any time in your dashboard under CMS, edit them, export them or delete them, with or without the app installed.

What they hold is business contact information about economic operators, which the GPSR requires to be shown publicly on the product page. That is why those collections are readable by anyone: the regulation asks for exactly that. If the manufacturer or the responsible person you enter is a natural person rather than a company, their name and contact details are personal data and you are the controller of it — you publish it because the law obliges you to.

2

What the app reads, and why

To do its job the app reads, but never keeps a copy of:

  • Your site's business details (name, address, e-mail), only to offer to fill in the manufacturer form when you are the manufacturer. Nothing is saved unless you press save.
  • Your store's products and categories, to list your catalogue so you can say which rule applies where. Only names, images and identifiers are read.
  • Your site's languages, to know which translations to ask you for.
3

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process any data about your shop's visitors.
  • It sets no cookies and uses no local storage on the shop's pages.
  • It uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • It sends data to no third party, and it uses no subprocessors.
  • It does not sell personal data.
  • It transfers no data outside the European Union: the data stays where Wix hosts your site.
4

How long the data is kept

For as long as you keep it. Uninstalling the app removes the app's access; the collections and their contents stay on your site until you delete them yourself in the CMS.

5

Your rights

Because the data sits on your own site under your own Wix account, you can read it, correct it, export it and erase it yourself at any time, without asking us. We hold no copy to correct or erase. If you have a question about this policy, write to the address in section 9.

6

Security

The data is stored by Wix, in Wix's own infrastructure, under the permissions set on each collection: only the site's editors can write, and only the information the GPSR requires to be public can be read by the public. The app is served over HTTPS and runs in your browser and on Wix's hosting. We operate no servers, no databases and no logs of our own.

7

Children

The app is a tool for running a shop. It is not directed at children and it collects no data from them.
